# Jailbreak Pattern Accessibility Research

TL;DR
This repository documents a research effort to evaluate the accessibility of published jailbreak patterns against contemporary large language models. The payloads and full transcripts are redacted; methodology, pattern analysis, and attribution are fully transparent.

Quick fact
- 30 minutes from Wired article to working exploit on DeepSeek (redacted evidence).

Contents
- RESEARCH.md — Full research report (sections 1–7, redacted where necessary)
- METHODOLOGY.md — Reproducible methodology (structure, instrumentation, success criteria)
- screenshots/ — Redacted evidence images (index inside)
- ATTRIBUTION.md — Credits and references
- LICENSE — MIT

Call to action
This repository is intended for security researchers, defenders, and threat modelers. Reproduce the pattern yourself using the redaction-safe methodology in METHODOLOGY.md; do not share operational payloads publicly.

Attribution
This work builds on publicly available reporting and prior research. Key credits: Wired (Burgess, April 2023), Polyakov / Adversa AI, DeepSeek platform testing, and Aletheia Sovereign Systems.

Safety & scope
- No unredacted operational payloads or full conversation transcripts are included.
- Screenshots are redacted to prevent operational reuse; evidence is documented and available to vetted parties under NDA.
- This repository is for research, defense, and academic discussion only.
