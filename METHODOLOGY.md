# Methodology

ASI-RESEARCH-001 · Editorial draft · Redaction-safe

## Research question

Can a publicly documented jailbreak pattern remain operational years after disclosure against a contemporary model?

## Separate evidence tracks

| Track | Purpose | Present status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persistence test | Assess the author's reported 2026 DeepSeek boundary crossing against a historical pattern family | Author-reported; EVIDENCE-001 and EVIDENCE-002 pending editorial addition |
| Consequence observation | Document a separate author-reported instance of weapons-related technical assistance without publishing dangerous content | Public support pending; EVIDENCE-003 optional, but the observation remains qualified if absent |
| External context | Interpret independent publications, not enroll their cases in the experiment | Exact references in ATTRIBUTION.md; not replication evidence |

## What is and is not recorded

DeepSeek is the tested platform named by the author. The supplied draft places the research in 2026. The earlier repository specified July 2026 without a supporting artifact. Treat that month as unverified historical metadata, not an established test date.

The public package does not yet establish the exact date/time, timezone, model/version, interface, settings, sample size, controls, run outcomes, or the basis for attributing the test to the historical pattern. Record unavailable fields as unavailable; do not infer a model version from the calendar.

Earlier descriptions of instrumentation, two independent runs, single-factor controls, and a 30-minute path to success were not supported by the repository artifacts. They are not completed procedures or results in this edition.

## Review procedure for existing evidence

1. Review authorized, existing research records in a controlled setting. This document does not request new harmful tests.
2. Assign distinct run identifiers to the persistence and consequence tracks. Preserve their dates and provenance separately.
3. Document the intended safety boundary and a non-operational description of the observed crossing. Establish the basis for the finding without releasing restricted content.
4. Explain at a non-operational level why the historical and tested cases belong to the same pattern family. Do not equate broad similarity with identical prompts.
5. Record actual run counts, control availability, negative results, uncertainty, and reviewer scope. A single screenshot does not establish repeatability.
6. Prepare safe artifacts using the screenshot rules, then record what each artifact supports and cannot support.

This is an evidence-review procedure, not a completed experiment log or a public replication guide.

## Success criteria and limits

The reported persistence outcome is a crossing of a defined safety boundary under the recorded conditions. Final editorial acceptance requires a reviewer to be able to substantiate that narrow statement from authorized evidence.

The separate consequence observation concerns the presence of weapons-related technical assistance. It does not test the correctness or practical effectiveness of that assistance. Do not attempt physical validation or publish technical instructions.

Anthropic's investigations and evaluations were conducted independently. They are neither control runs nor evidence of a shared jailbreak. Their inclusion supports context only.

## Safety and handling

Operational jailbreak prompts, turn-by-turn bypass instructions, full restricted outputs, personal identifiers, and dangerous weapons details are withheld. Use irreversible redaction in public exports, not blur or removable overlays. See [screenshot rules](screenshots/README.md).

Do not commit raw sensitive records, hidden layers, embedded text, or reversible redactions. The public repository does not establish a private archive or a working NDA/access process. Any future restricted review requires a separately agreed handling process.

## Editorial release gates

Before approving the article for a final PDF, confirm the exact metadata available, review EVIDENCE-001 and EVIDENCE-002, and verify the relationship to the historical source. Review EVIDENCE-003 or retain the explicit qualification of the separate observation; remove it if it cannot be responsibly supported.

Resolve the [evidence register](evidence/README.md), recheck external citations, and obtain author approval of the text. No AAC assignment is made in this edition: a pattern-specific mapping requires sufficient evidence and the current ASI taxonomy. No product evidence status changes follow from this work.
